Borrow: Your loan, your control.

A Progressive Web App for Arthmate giving borrowers direct access to their loan, payments, repayment schedule, NACH management, and overdue visibility, all without calling support.

The Brief

What Arthmate needed built

Arthmate needed a PWA MVP that centralises loan management for borrowers, covering login, loan overview, payment history, repayment schedule, NACH management, overdue handling, and customer support flows, all in one place, without a support call.

The users were existing borrowers of the organisation — people already familiar with loans, EMIs, and repayment cycles. They did not need education. What they needed was a single, clear place to see everything about their loan and take action on it. Every piece of information had to be transparent and accurate, and every action had to confirm what happened or, where applicable, tell them how long it would take to reflect.

The PWA approach was a deliberate choice. It allowed borrowers to install the app directly from their browser without going through an app store, lowering the friction to adoption. The design itself was mobile-first, because that is where borrowers actually interact with their loan. All of this had to be designed and handed over to development within a 6-week project window, with the design phase completed in 2.5 weeks.

Process

2.5 weeks of design inside a 6-week project

The project ran through 8 structured steps condensed into four phases. Each phase had a clear focus and output, keeping the team aligned without losing velocity.

01
Understand
Arthmate's loan management PWA primarily targets the borrowers of financial institutions that utilise Arthmate's lending platform. This audience consists of individuals and businesses who have availed loans through financial institutions powered by Arthmate's technology.
02
Define
Features from PRD
  • PAN-based sign in and authentication
  • Loan list with disbursement details and status
  • Statement of account and repayment schedule
  • ENACH management
  • Overdue and next due indicators
Benchmarking
KreditBee surfaced loan balances prominently but buried the repayment schedule three taps deep, most borrowers had never seen it. ICICI iPay had strong detail pages but no clear next action on overdue states. We combined both, the home screen shows the active loan card with outstanding balance and next due date, with Pay Now reachable in a single tap.
Persona
These were not new-to-finance users, they were existing borrowers of the organisation, people who had already taken a loan and now needed a place to manage it. What they needed was simple: complete transparency on every piece of information about their loan, and clear feedback on every action they took. If a payment was made, confirm it. If a NACH update would take 3 working days to reflect, say so. No ambiguity, no guessing. That expectation shaped every screen, every status state, and every confirmation message in the product.
03
Design
Built the full information architecture covering all 5 modules before a single visual was created. With five interconnected modules and a 2.5-week design window, committing to the sitemap first meant any missed flow was caught before it became a visual design rework. One signed-off IA meant the rest of the process could move fast without revisiting structure. The sitemap was reviewed and approved with Product and stakeholders before wireframing began.
04
Deliver
Wireframes created in iterations and validated with Product. High-fidelity designs built across all 5 modules and finalised with the marketing team before dev handover.
Outcome

Complete design handover, on time.

The full PWA MVP, all 5 modules, was designed and handed over to the development team within the 2.5-week design window. Every screen, flow, and edge state was documented and ready for build. The handover included login and authentication, loan overview, repayment schedule, NACH management, overdue handling, and customer support flows, with zero open design questions left for the engineering team to resolve.

Learnings

What I took away

Designing for financial clarity
When users are managing their own money, clarity is the feature. Plain labels, explicit confirmation states, and honest timelines on pending actions reduced more friction than any visual refinement. The moment an action happens without feedback, the user assumes something went wrong.
Tight timelines need ruthless scope
With design completing in 2.5 weeks of a 6-week project, every feature had to earn its place. Returning to the PRD daily kept the team honest about what was truly in and what wasn't.
PWA ≠ native app
PWAs have real interaction constraints. Benchmarking PWA Today early in the process prevented significant rework during the visual design phase.
Cross-functional from day one
Involving Product, Marketing, and Dev from the requirements phase meant zero late-stage surprises during handover. Early alignment is the real deliverable.
