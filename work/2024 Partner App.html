The Brief

Scattered, manual, and hard to control.

The client had an existing application for their dealer and distributor network, but it was riddled with loopholes. Operations were fragmented across phone calls, spreadsheets, and informal channels. There was no single source of truth, no real visibility into what partners were doing, and a significant portion of activity falling outside standard business practices.

The goal was to build one governed digital platform that covers it all, sales registration, rewards, network management, product discovery, and partner support. One app to replace the fragmented status quo.

Process

Eight to ten months of parallel momentum.

There was no luxury of a long discovery-first approach. The engagement was sprint-driven by necessity, the client had a vision, and the pace demanded we keep moving forward while staying precise.

01
Understand

Joined the project at the flow and architecture stage. Reviewing the early discovery output revealed where the existing app was breaking down, sales registrations had no guard against duplicate entries, distributors had no view of their dealer network's activity, and the rewards programme ran entirely on manual tracking outside the app. These failure points directly shaped the design priority: governance and traceability had to come before engagement. Every module was mapped with that lens before any visual work began.

02
Design

Worked directly with a junior designer to concept and execute each module. Led brainstorming, set direction, and refined execution together. Presented designs to the client, gathered feedback, and iterated through multiple rounds until each screen met the brief and earned sign-off.

03
Handover

Signed-off modules were handed to the development team in parallel. Maintained ongoing communication with dev throughout, answering queries, resolving edge cases, and adapting designs when technical constraints surfaced mid-build, without losing timeline.

04
Launch

The initial phase of the application went live in 2024 on both iOS and Android. Design and development crossed the finish line together, on time, to client satisfaction, and ready for real-world use by the partner network.

What We Built

Nine modules. One super application.

The Partner App was not a single-purpose tool. It was a full-stack partner experience covering everything a dealer or distributor needed to do their job, from selling products to managing their network, tracking rewards, and getting support.

Sign In
Secure onboarding for existing dealers and distributors. No open sign-ups, access was controlled and credentialled from day one.
iSmart
The gamification engine. Partners earned rewards, cash balance, and exclusive trips based on sales performance, all tracked in real time.
Sales Registration
Register product sales digitally, replacing manual logging and enabling accurate tracking of partner performance.
Product Catalogue
Browse the full product range, check specs, and share product details with customers directly from the app, no printed catalogue needed.
Network Management
Distributors can view, track and manage their entire dealer base from one screen, with real visibility into partner activity and performance.
Battery Management
Scan, register and track battery products with warranty verification built in, replacing a process that was previously done on paper or not at all.
Create Advertisement
Partners could create their own co-branded advertisements directly within the app, personalised, on-brand marketing at the local level.
Reports
Performance reports and business summaries accessible directly by partners, no more waiting for the client to share data manually.
Help & Support
In-app support and notification centre keeping partners informed and connected without needing to pick up a phone.

Outcome

Live. Adopted. Trusted.

The initial phase of the Partner App launched in 2024 on both the App Store and Google Play. What started as a brief to digitise manual operations became a full-scale super application that the partner network now relies on daily.

All four objectives from the brief were met. Centralise, one governed platform replaced the fragmented mix of calls, spreadsheets, and informal channels. Digitise, sales registration, battery management, and reporting moved fully off paper. Control, the client gained real visibility into partner activity and network performance for the first time. Engage, the iSmart rewards engine tied partner motivation directly to measurable sales outcomes.

Learnings

What this project taught me.

Design and dev are one team
Working in parallel with development wasn't just a workflow, it was a mindset shift. The real skill wasn't designing faster, it was staying connected with what dev needed and adapting without derailing either side.
Strong opinions require stronger listening
The client had a clear, fixed vision. Navigating that required understanding the intent behind every request before responding with design. Pushback only worked when it was backed by a better answer, not just a preference.
Mentoring through complexity
Directing a junior through nine modules of varying complexity meant translating ambiguity into clarity for someone else first. It sharpened how I communicate design decisions, not just what, but why.
Shipping at scale changes what "done" means
Nine modules across two platforms, running in parallel with development, taught me that knowing when a screen is finished, not perfect, is a design skill in itself. Every hour spent refining past sign-off was an hour taken from a module still waiting to be started.
